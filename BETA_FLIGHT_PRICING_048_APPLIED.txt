iumrah Beta update 048

Flight search
- Trip Builder asks for round-trip or one-way before the search.
- Ignav receives a broad one- or two-leg search without airline, stop, baggage,
  time-window, maximum-price or self-transfer exclusions.
- Every returned Ignav itinerary keeps its provider identity and stays available
  in the results list.
- Results can be sorted by recommendation, price or duration and filtered by
  stops and airline inside the app.
- Round-trip cards show both legs and the complete current party fare clearly.

Package pricing
- One selected provider itinerary is priced exactly once.
- The current hotel components and every package cost component are embedded in
  the booking as a generator pricing snapshot.
- The displayed package price is explicitly indicative, not final-confirmed.
- The existing 50 percent markup, 2 percent payment fee and public rounding are
  recorded in the snapshot for iumrah Business.

Operations
- Successful Ignav responses increment the shared monthly D1 usage counter.
- Configure IGNAV_MONTHLY_REQUEST_BUDGET if the deployment budget is not 3000.

Required deployment order
1. Apply this patch to the Beta repository.
2. Deploy Backend/PackageEngine.
3. Build and distribute the iOS application.
