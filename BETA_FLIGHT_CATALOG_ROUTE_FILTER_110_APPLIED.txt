iumrah Beta — flight catalogue + generator route filter update 110
Date: 2026-09-10

What changed
- Generator published-flight rows now respect the pilgrim's selected route exactly:
  * Makkah only: origin → JED and JED → origin.
  * Makkah + Madinah, first airport MED: origin → MED and JED → origin.
  * Makkah + Madinah, first airport JED: origin → JED and MED → origin.
- Selecting a published card no longer silently rewrites the selected package scope/arrival airport.
- Route labels show the exact outbound and return airports instead of JED / MED placeholders.
- The Hotels > Flights storefront no longer truncates the visible catalogue to 12 cards.
- The public storefront flight board now returns all future staff-published direct Umrah routes (up to 300 rows), while `origin` remains the hotel-package baseline selector.
- The generator fallback ignores complete itineraries belonging to a different departure city.
