iumrah Beta — Hotel Storefront build fix 094
Date: 2026-09-06

Fixes Xcode simulator build failure from hotel storefront v1:
Sources/Views/Tabs/HotelsHomeView.swift: invalid redeclaration of 'body'.

Cause:
ShowcaseHero declared both a stored String property named `body` and SwiftUI View's required computed `body` property.

Fix:
The hero text property is renamed to `description`, and all three ShowcaseHero call sites are updated accordingly.
No pricing, backend, cache, navigation, or design behavior is changed by this hotfix.
