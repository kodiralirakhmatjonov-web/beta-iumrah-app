TestFlight signing is intentionally NOT stored in update ZIPs.

The beta repository needs:
  Signing/distribution-private-key.enc
  Signing/<profile-for-com.iumrah.app>.mobileprovision

Required repository secrets:
  APPLE_TEAM_ID
  APPLE_ID_EMAIL
  APPLE_APP_SPECIFIC_PASSWORD
  IOS_SIGNING_KEY_PASSWORD

Important: iumrah-business.mobileprovision is for com.iumrah.business and cannot sign com.iumrah.app.
The TestFlight workflow checks the provisioning profile bundle ID and private-key/certificate match before archiving.
